Given:

- Property Capital owned by you individually;
- Australian tax resident;
- No employment income;
- Taxable income generated by the portfolio;
- Approximately AUD 50k carried-forward capital losses;

the research should be run as one linked program.

Capital losses affect transition trades, but **do not offset interest, dividends, or other ordinary income**. The after-tax yield model must calculate your tax progressively because Property Capital itself will create most of your taxable income.

# Research program

## Phase 1 — Establish the tax and income baseline

This must come first because gross yields cannot be compared meaningfully without it.

### 1.1 Inventory expected portfolio income

For every existing holding, collect:

- Expected annual cash distributions;
- Franked and unfranked components;
- Expected franking credits;
- Foreign dividends;
- Foreign withholding tax;
- Interest distributions;
- Return-of-capital components;
- Reliability and variability of the distribution.

Pay particular attention to:

- `SOL`
- `BHP`
- `VAS`
- `VGS`
- `VGE`
- `CRED`
- `FLBL`
- `JPST`
- `UKWl`
- Government-bond coupons

Use both trailing 12-month income and a conservative forward estimate. BHP’s latest dividend should not simply be annualized because it is cyclical.

### 1.2 Build the personal tax model

Model taxable income from:

1. Australian interest;
2. Australian unfranked distributions;
3. Franked dividends plus grossed-up franking credits;
4. Foreign dividends and interest;
5. Foreign tax offsets;
6. Realized capital gains;
7. Carried-forward capital losses;
8. Applicable CGT discounts;
9. Medicare levy and relevant offsets.

Calculate tax at multiple Property Capital gross yields:

- 3.5%
- 4.0%
- 4.5%
- 5.0%
- 5.5%

The effective tax rate is endogenous: earning more interest moves part of the income through higher marginal bands.

### 1.3 Determine the true fixed-income income requirement

Start with:

- Household deficit: AUD 70,596 annually.

Then deduct conservatively expected after-tax Long-Term portfolio income.

For example:

```text
Household deficit
− reliable after-tax SOL income
− reliable after-tax BHP income
− other after-tax distributions
= income required from Property Capital
```

Produce two cases:

- **Core case:** includes conservative expected portfolio distributions.
- **Defensive case:** assumes equity distributions fall materially.

The Property Capital yield target should cover the core case. Operating Capital protects the defensive case.

### Deliverable

A tax and income table showing:

| Gross Property yield | Property income | Other portfolio income | Tax | Net household income | Deficit/surplus |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

# Phase 2 — Research eligible markets

These lanes can run in parallel, but all rates must be captured on approximately the same date.

## Lane A — At-call deposits

### Research

For each candidate account:

- Headline interest rate;
- Base and bonus components;
- Maximum balance receiving the advertised rate;
- Deposit, transaction, or growth conditions;
- Rate expiry or introductory period;
- Interest-payment frequency;
- Account ownership requirements;
- Withdrawal limits;
- Underlying ADI;
- FCS treatment;
- Shared banking licence with other brands.

### Sources

- Bank rate and product pages;
- Product disclosure statements;
- APRA’s ADI register;
- Australian Government FCS information.

### Output

Rank by:

1. Effective rate on the actual proposed balance;
2. Conditions and operational burden;
3. FCS-covered amount;
4. Speed of withdrawal.

Do not compare using headline rates where only a small balance qualifies.

## Lane B — Term deposits

### Research

Collect rates for maturities around:

- 3 months;
- 6 months;
- April 2027;
- June–July 2027;
- 12 months;
- 18 months.

For each:

- Annual rate;
- Compounding basis;
- Interest-payment timing;
- Minimum and maximum deposit;
- Early-break notice period;
- Break penalty;
- FCS treatment;
- Automatic rollover rules;
- Maturity instructions;
- Effective after-tax annual return.

### Decision question

Does the term deposit offer enough additional after-tax return over at-call deposits or Commonwealth securities to compensate for reduced flexibility?

### Output

A maturity ladder proposal using distinct ADIs where the FCS benefit is material.

## Lane C — Commonwealth government securities

### Research

For each relevant maturity:

- Current bid and offer price;
- Coupon;
- Yield to maturity;
- Modified duration;
- Accrued interest;
- Maturity proceeds;
- Brokerage and bid/ask spread;
- Expected value at possible purchase dates;
- Tax treatment of coupons, discount, premium, sale, and redemption.

Cover maturities around:

- April 2027;
- 2028;
- 2029;
- 2030;
- 2031–2033.

Include existing:

- `GSBG27.AX`
- `GSBG33.AX`

### Sources

- Australian Office of Financial Management;
- ASX/eAGB information;
- RBA yield data;
- Executable broker quotes;
- Australian tax guidance or accountant confirmation.

### Output

A government ladder showing:

| Maturity | YTM | Duration | Allocation | Annual income | Expected maturity value |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|

Direct maturity-matched bonds should be compared with government-bond ETFs. ETFs offer diversification and operational simplicity but do not provide a fixed portfolio maturity.

## Lane D — Semi-government and AAA-quality AUD securities

### Research

For candidate state-government and comparable high-grade securities:

- Issuer;
- Maturity;
- Credit rating;
- Yield to maturity;
- Spread over equivalent Commonwealth maturity;
- Duration;
- Bid/ask spread;
- Daily trading liquidity;
- Minimum parcel;
- Callable or non-callable status;
- Tax treatment.

### Key comparison

Calculate:

```text
After-tax semi-government yield
− after-tax Commonwealth yield
= compensation for incremental issuer and liquidity risk
```

Prefer semis where that spread is meaningful and market liquidity is adequate.

### Output

A shortlist across several issuers and maturities, avoiding concentration in one state or issuance line.

---

## Lane E — Investment-grade credit

### Research

For `CRED.AX` and alternatives:

- Yield to worst—not merely distribution yield;
- Effective duration;
- Weighted credit rating;
- Issuer concentration;
- Financial-sector concentration;
- Floating versus fixed-rate exposure;
- Management fee;
- Bid/ask spread;
- Historical drawdown during credit stress;
- Distribution tax character;
- Underlying maturity profile;
- Exposure to subordinated debt;
- Exposure to hybrids;
- Currency hedging.

### Exclusions

Do not admit an instrument to Property Capital merely because it is labeled “income” or “bond.”

Exclude or heavily penalize:

- Lower-quality credit;
- Leveraged loans;
- Subordinated financial debt;
- Hybrids with equity-like loss behavior;
- Unhedged foreign currency;
- Products whose advertised yield relies on return of capital.

### Output

Rank candidates by:

```text
After-tax yield to worst
− management fee
− estimated stress-loss cost
```

Cap the final Property credit sleeve at 15%.

---

## Lane F — Inflation-linked securities

### Research

For Australian indexed bonds or funds:

- Real yield;
- Maturity;
- Modified duration;
- Indexation mechanism;
- Inflation lag;
- Break-even inflation versus nominal Commonwealth bonds;
- Tax treatment of coupon and principal indexation;
- Liquidity;
- Value under rising and falling real yields.

### Decision question

Does the current real yield justify the duration risk, and does the instrument improve protection against property-cost growth?

### Output

Recommend 0–10%. Zero is acceptable if real yields, duration, or tax treatment are unattractive.

# Phase 3 — Research the transition

## 3.1 Foreign-exchange execution

After repaying EUR 150k, compare conversion of the residual EUR through:

- Interactive Brokers;
- Wise;
- Any Australian bank option worth considering.

Measure:

- Quoted EUR/AUD rate;
- Spread from the institutional mid-rate;
- Explicit fee;
- Transfer fee;
- Maximum transaction size;
- Settlement timing;
- Withdrawal timing;
- Total AUD received.

Use the same reference time for comparison.

The goal is operational efficiency, not forecasting EUR/AUD.

## 3.2 Existing foreign fixed income

Assess:

### `JPST`

- Current AUD tax cost base;
- Current AUD capital gain/loss;
- Sale spread and commission;
- Expected yield if retained;
- AUD FX risk;
- Best AUD replacement.

Likely outcome: sell and replace.

### `FLBL`

- Current AUD tax loss;
- Forward yield after US withholding and Australian tax;
- Credit quality and default exposure;
- Stress drawdown;
- Role, if any, in Long-Term Capital.

Likely outcome: reduce or sell, but decide from forward return rather than merely its current loss.

### `UKWl`

- Keep separate from Property Capital.
- Review as a Long-Term investment only.
- Do not sell solely to generate a tax loss.

## 3.3 Capital-loss schedule

Create a tax-lot schedule showing:

- Approximate AUD 50k carried loss;
- Loss available from `JPST`;
- Loss available from `FLBL`;
- Loss available from `UKWl` if independently sold;
- Gains from any other proposed disposals;
- Short-term versus discount-eligible gains.

Do not use capital losses to justify an otherwise unattractive sale. They only reduce the friction of a strategically justified transaction.

# Phase 6 — Stress testing

Run at least:

### Rate shocks

- Government yields +1%;
- Government yields +1.5%;
- Government yields +2%;
- Government yields −1%.

### Credit shocks

- Credit spreads +1%;
- Credit spreads +2%;
- Credit spreads +3%.

### Combined stress

- Government yields +1.5%;
- Credit spreads +2%;
- Equity portfolio −35%;
- No favorable FX movement;
- Equity dividends reduced;
- BHP dividend materially reduced.

### Purchase-date scenarios

Assume the entire requirement is needed in:

- August 2027;
- August 2028;
- August 2029;
- August 2030.

Calculate realized proceeds after spreads and sale costs—not just statement value.

### Required result

- Property Capital remains at least 95% of target under the prescribed market stress;
- Any shortfall is covered without borrowing or forced equity sales;
- Operating Capital remains intact.
